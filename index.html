VERTICALVALET

Your parking spot is on the side of a skyscraper. Drive up the walls. Park it.

PLAY Free, in your browser · phone or desktop · no download

THE GAME

A sports car with rocket thrusters. A city that's all walls.

Your car's tyres stick to anything. Drive straight into a building and you're driving up it. Fire the thrusters to leap from tower to tower, climb higher, and finish with the hard part: a precise park in a garage bay hanging 246 metres above the street.

Fuel is limited and refills while you drive. Score comes from time, fuel left and how cleanly you park. Three in-car radio stations keep you company.

HOW TO PLAY

The idea

  1. Drive into a wall and the car sticks to it. Climb.
  2. Jump off a wall or roof; hold jump in the air to fire the lift thrusters.
  3. Glass gives no grip. Go around it.
  4. Roll into the glowing bay and stop on the mark, nose in. PARKED. HOME.

Desktop

W A S Ddrive and steer (or the arrow keys) Spacetap to jump, hold for lift thrusters Shiftrocket boost Qchange radio station Rrestart the level Escpause
